**WhisperCharacter** makes the Whisper option in the Battle.net friend menu message your friend's **character** instead of their Battle.net account.

## Features

- Right-click a Battle.net friend, then **Whisper**: opens a whisper to the character they are currently logged in on.
- Hold **Shift** while clicking **Whisper** for the regular Battle.net whisper.
- Falls back to the regular Battle.net whisper when the friend is offline, in another game, or on a different WoW version or region.
- No extra menu entries and no settings.

## Installation

Extract to `Interface\AddOns\` (folder name must be `WhisperCharacter`) and `/reload`.

## Notes

- Whispers use the character name without the realm, so characters on non-connected realms may not be reachable.
- Cross-faction whispers depend on what the game allows.
